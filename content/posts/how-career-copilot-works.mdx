Career Copilot is designed to be simple. Here's how it works in three steps.

## Step 1: Tell Us About Yourself

Share your background — your education, work experience, skills, and what kind of role you're looking for. Career Copilot uses this to understand where you are in your career journey.

## Step 2: Get Your Personalized Plan

Our AI, powered by Google Gemini, analyzes your profile and creates a tailored career roadmap. This includes recommended job titles, skills to develop, and concrete next steps.

## Step 3: Practice & Improve

Use our AI interview simulator to practice answering real interview questions. Get instant feedback on your responses, body language tips, and suggestions for improvement. Track your progress over time.

---

Career Copilot is available 24/7 and adapts to your schedule. Whether you have 10 minutes or an hour, every session moves you closer to your goal.
