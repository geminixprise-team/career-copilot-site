Career Copilot is built for anyone who wants professional career guidance without the high price tag of a traditional career coach.

## Students & Fresh Graduates

Starting your career can be overwhelming. Career Copilot helps you translate your education into a compelling job application, practice for your first interviews, and identify industries that match your skills.

## Job Seekers & Unemployed Professionals

If you're between roles, Career Copilot helps you stay focused and strategic. Get a clear action plan, keep your interview skills sharp, and discover opportunities you might not have considered.

## Career Changers

Thinking about switching industries? Career Copilot maps your transferable skills to new career paths and helps you build a bridge from where you are to where you want to be.

---

No matter your background, Career Copilot meets you where you are and helps you get to where you want to go.
