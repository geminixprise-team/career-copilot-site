Interviewing doesn't have to be terrifying. Here are five practical tips that will help you perform better in any interview.

## 1. Research the Company Deeply

Go beyond the "About Us" page. Read their recent blog posts, press releases, and LinkedIn activity. Mention something specific in your interview to show genuine interest.

## 2. Use the STAR Method

When answering behavioral questions, structure your response: **Situation**, **Task**, **Action**, **Result**. This keeps your answers focused and memorable.

## 3. Prepare Questions to Ask

Having thoughtful questions shows engagement. Ask about team dynamics, growth opportunities, or what success looks like in the first 90 days.

## 4. Practice Out Loud

Reading answers in your head is not the same as saying them. Practice with a friend, in front of a mirror, or use Career Copilot's AI interview simulator for realistic practice with instant feedback.

## 5. Follow Up

Send a brief thank-you email within 24 hours. Reference something specific from the conversation to make it personal.
