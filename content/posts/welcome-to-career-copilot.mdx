We're building **Career Copilot** — an AI-powered career coaching platform that gives you personalized guidance, realistic interview practice, and a clear roadmap to your next role.

## What is Career Copilot?

Career Copilot uses **Google Gemini** to deliver one-on-one career coaching that adapts to your unique situation. Whether you're a student entering the job market, someone between roles, or looking to make a career switch — we meet you where you are.

## What You'll Get

- **Personalized Career Roadmaps** — A step-by-step plan based on your skills, experience, and goals
- **AI Mock Interviews** — Practice realistic interviews with instant feedback on your answers
- **Resume & Cover Letter Reviews** — Get actionable suggestions to make your application stand out
- **Job Search Strategy** — Tailored advice on where to look, how to network, and when to apply
