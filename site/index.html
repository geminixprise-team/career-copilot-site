Features

Everything You Need to Land the Role

From strategy to execution, Career Copilot covers the full career-development journey.

Personalized Career Roadmaps

AI-generated step-by-step plans based on your unique background, skills, and target role. No generic advice — only what's relevant to you.

Realistic Interview Practice

Simulate real interviews with AI-powered mock sessions. Get instant feedback on your answers, delivery, and confidence.

Gamified Progress Tracking

Earn XP, unlock levels, and compete with AI rivals. Gamification keeps you motivated and accountable every day.

Portfolio Project Generator

Get custom project ideas tailored to your target industry. Build proof of skill that stands out to hiring managers.

Daily Coaching Check-Ins

Proof-Based Task Completion

Submit screenshots, links, or text as proof of completed tasks. The AI validates your work and moves you forward.
