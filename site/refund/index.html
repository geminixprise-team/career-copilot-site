Refund Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2025

1. Overview

We want you to be satisfied with Career Copilot. This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which you may request a refund for paid subscriptions.

2. Payment Processing

All payments for Career Copilot are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. Paddle handles billing, tax collection, and payment processing on our behalf. Your purchase receipt and invoice will come from Paddle.

3. Refund Eligibility

You may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase or subscription renewal, provided that:

  • You have not extensively used the premium features during this period
  • You submit your refund request through the proper channel (see below)

After the 14-day period, refunds are not available for the current billing cycle. You may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Visit our contact page and select "Refund Request" as the subject
  2. Include your account email address and the reason for your request
  3. We will process your request within 5 business days

Alternatively, you may contact Paddle directly through the purchase receipt email you received.

5. Refund Method

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Refunds are processed by Paddle and may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your payment provider.

6. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Upon cancellation:

  • You retain access to premium features until the end of your current billing period
  • No further charges will be made after the current period ends
  • Your account reverts to the free tier (if available) at the end of the billing period

7. Exceptions

Refunds may not be granted in cases of:

  • Violation of our Terms of Service
  • Abuse of the refund policy (e.g., repeated subscription and refund cycles)
  • Requests made after the 14-day refund window

8. Free Trial and Waitlist

If you are on our free waitlist or using a free trial, no payment has been collected. No refund is applicable. You can unsubscribe from our waitlist communications at any time.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

For refund-related questions, please visit our contact page.
